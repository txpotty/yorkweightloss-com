    What Wegovy Is in Harrisburg

    Wegovy is brand-name semaglutide made by Novo Nordisk and FDA-approved for chronic weight management. Each pen holds one weekly dose, with a hidden needle that pops out on press. No measuring is needed. Ozempic contains the same ingredient but is FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes, so Wegovy is the correct prescription when weight management is the goal. An oral Wegovy tablet is also FDA-approved for patients who prefer pills.

    Who Wegovy Is For

    Adults with a BMI of 30 or higher, or 27 or higher with a weight-related condition, are the usual candidates. Qualifying conditions include high blood pressure, high cholesterol, prediabetes and sleep apnea. Teens can qualify too. The heart risk reduction label applies to adults with heart disease and a BMI of 27 or more. It is not for type 1 diabetes, and your provider weighs every factor against your full health history before writing a prescription.

    Wegovy Dosing Schedule in Harrisburg

    Wegovy starts at 0.25 mg weekly and climbs through four more monthly steps until it reaches 2.4 mg. Each step usually lasts four weeks. Pick a weekday and keep it, morning or evening, fed or fasting. The pen goes under the skin of the belly or thigh, and moving the spot each week cuts down on redness and small bumps under the skin. A missed dose can be taken within five days, or skipped if the next one is under two days away.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Weeks one to four at 0.25 mg are a warm-up, and some people notice only a slightly smaller appetite. Nausea often shows up in the first two days after a shot. The 1 mg step often feels different. By month three, many patients stop at half a plate without trying. In STEP 1, weight loss kept building through about week 60 before leveling off, so the first eight weeks are only a small part of the picture.

    Wegovy Side Effects in Harrisburg

    Nausea, diarrhea, constipation and vomiting are the most reported side effects, and they tend to flare in the week after a step up. Smaller meals with less grease help a lot. Hydrate well. Less common risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, kidney problems from dehydration and low blood sugar when Wegovy is paired with insulin or a sulfonylurea. A boxed warning covers thyroid C-cell tumors found in rodent studies.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The intake asks about a personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN2, which rules Wegovy out entirely. Your provider also looks for past pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, diabetic retinopathy, severe stomach emptying problems and any history of an eating disorder before writing a prescription. Pregnancy is a hard stop. A metabolic panel from the past year can strengthen an insurance request.
