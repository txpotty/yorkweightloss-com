    What Causes Low Libido in Lancaster

    Low libido in women rarely has a single cause. Antidepressants, the pill, perimenopause, thyroid issues, stress, bad sleep, painful sex and relationship tension all show up often. So does how you feel in your body. Persistent, distressing low desire without another explanation is called hypoactive sexual desire disorder, or HSDD. That label opens up FDA-approved options.

    Who Low Libido Treatment Is For

    This is for adult women bothered by a drop in desire or arousal. It often helps women whose interest changed after starting a medication, having a baby, entering perimenopause or going through a stressful stretch at work or home. No diagnosis is needed first. Women who are simply not bothered by lower desire do not need treatment, since HSDD is defined partly by distress. Your provider helps sort it out.

    Treatment Options and Schedules in Lancaster

    Flibanserin, sold as Addyi, is a 100 mg tablet taken every night at bedtime. PT-141, brand name Vyleesi, is a 1.75 mg self-injection taken at least 45 minutes before sex, with limits of once a day and eight times a month. Oxytocin is a compounded option. It is used off-label as a spray or troche before intimacy and is not FDA-approved. Some women need no medication at all.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly review. Your provider may suggest changes to medications, sleep or treatment of vaginal dryness before or alongside any new prescription, since those fixes can help on their own. PT-141 effects show up within hours. Flibanserin builds over four to eight weeks, and providers usually stop it at eight weeks if nothing has improved. Check-ins track the change.

    Side Effects in Lancaster

    Flibanserin can cause dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, fatigue and low blood pressure. Alcohol and certain interacting drugs make them stronger, which is why it is taken at bedtime with drinking limits. Nausea is common with PT-141. Flushing, headache, injection site redness, a short blood pressure bump and skin darkening with frequent use are also possible. Oxytocin can cause headache.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Liver disease rules out flibanserin. It is also avoided with moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitors such as fluconazole, clarithromycin or some HIV medications, and alcohol must be limited near bedtime. Blood pressure matters for PT-141. Uncontrolled high blood pressure and known heart disease generally rule PT-141 out. Neither is used during pregnancy.

    Comparing Low Libido Options in Lancaster

    Flibanserin is daily and steady. It tends to suit women who rarely drink and prefer one routine pill, though the average benefit in trials was modest, around one extra satisfying sexual event per month compared with placebo. PT-141 is as-needed. Some women prefer that, though nausea can be a drawback. Oxytocin is gentler, with limited research.
