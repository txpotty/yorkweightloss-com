    Semaglutide (the active ingredient in Wegovy and Ozempic) works on the GLP-1 receptor. Tirzepatide (Zepbound and Mounjaro) works on both GLP-1 and GIP receptors and produced larger average weight loss in clinical trials. Your provider recommends one based on your history, goals and tolerance.

    Compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide are prepared by licensed pharmacies to a provider prescription. They are not FDA-approved products, so we only work with pharmacies that meet state and federal compounding standards and test their batches. Your provider will explain the difference and help you choose.

    Pricing depends on the medication, form and dose your provider recommends, and is shown to you before you commit to anything. There is no charge for the questionnaire.

    GLP-1s are not appropriate for people with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2, people who are pregnant or breastfeeding, and some people with a history of pancreatitis or certain eye conditions. The questionnaire screens for these and a provider makes the final call.
